        Built at AOE

        We build out of AOE Robotics Club in Rockville Town Center, one of the largest robotics clubs in the Maryland, Virginia and D.C. area. AOE gives students whose schools have no robotics program a place to compete.

        The club is run by Weestar, a nonprofit. Its mentors are industry professionals and educators, and alongside its V5RC teams it runs VEX IQ and FTC teams.

        AOE also hosts tournaments of its own, including the AOE Robotics Challenge in the fall and the AOE Robotics Summit in late winter. We play in both.

        About the team

        Who we are

        We are the Lucky Cats Robotics Team, a nonprofit student-led organization based in Rockville, Maryland. Our team is made up of curious, creative, and dedicated students who share a passion for robotics, engineering, and technology. Together, we design, build, and program robots to compete in the VEX Robotics Competition, one of the world's largest and fastest-growing STEM competitions.

        What we do

        Each season, we take on new engineering challenges where we design robots from the ground up. We brainstorm, prototype, program, and test until our robots are ready for competition. Beyond building robots, we:

        • Run STEM outreach programs to introduce robotics to younger students
        • Share our knowledge with peers and mentor future teams
        • Encourage diversity and inclusion in robotics so everyone feels welcome

        Our mission

        To inspire the next generation of innovators. Robotics isn't just about competing. It's about learning, teaching, and giving back.

        1. 01Develop real-world skills in engineering, coding, and problem-solving
        2. 02Create a fun, supportive environment that celebrates collaboration and innovation
        3. 03Teach and inspire others in our community to explore STEM opportunities
        4. 04Represent our school and community with pride at local, regional, and national competitions

        招き猫

        Where the name comes from

        Our name, the Lucky Cats, represents our belief in teamwork, determination, and just a little bit of luck to achieve big goals. Like the waving Lucky Cat (maneki-neko) symbol, we aim to bring good fortune, not only to our team but to everyone we inspire along the way.

        the coin in its paw reads 大吉: “great luck”
