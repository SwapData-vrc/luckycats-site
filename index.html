        06AOE Robotics

        Built at AOE

        We build out of AOE Robotics Club in Rockville Town Center, one of the largest robotics clubs in the Maryland, Virginia and D.C. area. AOE gives students whose schools have no robotics program a place to compete.

        The club is run by Weestar, a nonprofit. Its mentors are industry professionals and educators, and alongside its V5RC teams it runs VEX IQ and FTC teams.

        AOE also hosts tournaments of its own: the AOE Robotics Challenge in the fall and the AOE Robotics Summit in late winter. We compete in both.

        aoerobotics.com

        Why 20850
        Every AOE V5 team number starts with 20850, Rockville's ZIP code. The letter is the team: we are C.

        07About

        About the team

        We are the Lucky Cats Robotics Team, a nonprofit student-led organization based in Rockville, Maryland.

        Our team is made up of curious, creative, and dedicated students who share a passion for robotics, engineering, and technology. Together, we design, build, and program robots to compete in the VEX Robotics Competition, one of the world's largest and fastest-growing STEM competitions.

        Each season, we take on new engineering challenges where we design robots from the ground up. We brainstorm, prototype, program, and test until our robots are ready for competition. Beyond building robots, we run STEM outreach programs for younger students, share our knowledge with peers and mentor future teams, and encourage diversity and inclusion in robotics so everyone feels welcome.

        Our mission

        To inspire the next generation of innovators. Robotics isn't just about competing. It's about learning, teaching, and giving back.

        1. Develop real-world skills in engineering, coding, and problem-solving
        2. Create a fun, supportive environment that celebrates collaboration and innovation
        3. Teach and inspire others in our community to explore STEM opportunities
        4. Represent our school and community with pride at local, regional, and national competitions
