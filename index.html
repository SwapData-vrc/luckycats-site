        06AOE Robotics

        Where we build

        We're part of AOE Robotics Club in Rockville Town Center. It's one of the biggest robotics clubs around Maryland, Virginia and D.C., and it's open to students whose schools don't have a robotics team.

        The club is run by Weestar, a nonprofit. The coaches are engineers and teachers, and besides V5 there are VEX IQ and FTC teams too.

        AOE also runs its own tournaments, the AOE Robotics Challenge in the fall and the AOE Robotics Summit in February. We go to both.

        aoerobotics.com

        Why 20850?
        Every AOE V5 team number starts with 20850, which is Rockville's ZIP code. The letter after it is the team. We're C.
        Across the club

        07About

        Who we are

        We're the Lucky Cats, a student-run nonprofit robotics team in Rockville, Maryland.

        Every season we design, build and code a new robot for the VEX Robotics Competition. The game changes each year, so we start from scratch: brainstorm, prototype, test, fix whatever broke, and keep going until it's ready to compete.

        Outside of competitions we teach robotics to younger kids, help newer teams get started, and try to make sure anyone who wants to try robotics feels welcome on a team.

        Our mission

        Get more kids into engineering. Winning is fun, but we care just as much about learning, teaching and giving back.

        1. Learn real engineering, coding and problem-solving
        2. Keep the team fun and make sure everyone has each other's back
        3. Teach younger students and get them interested in STEM
        4. Represent Rockville well at every competition we go to
